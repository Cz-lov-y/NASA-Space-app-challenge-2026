# Preliminary combined global series

This is real NASA GLDAS_NOAH025_M.2.1 model data and MOD13A3.061 data processed in Google Earth Engine, not synthetic data. It is preliminary and not yet validated for scientific interpretation. The region is the whole-Earth EPSG:4326 rectangle; both valid-area fractions use total Earth area, including oceans. The two datasets observe/model different footprints; the fractions do not quantify overlap. Discuss coverage differences with the team before interpreting paired trends. All present observations have `coverage_review_required`; no global coverage threshold has been chosen.

The NDVI source is the monthly 1 km MODIS Terra product, using raw NDVI scale 0.0001, source masks, valid raw range −2000..10000, `(DetailedQA & 3)==0` and `SummaryQA==0`. Earth Engine applies native-grid area weighting prior to export; an independent global calculation remains outstanding. The Google-hosted asset IDs, projection, QA rule, coverage and configuration times remain in the raw yearly CSVs; those CSVs are aggregate outputs, not local archives of source rasters. The soil data are model-estimated; `soil_water_0_10cm` and summed `soil_water_0_100cm` are both in kg m-2. See `data/processed/global_gldas/metadata.md` for GLDAS processing and source checksums, and `docs/modis_download_options.md` for MODIS limitations.

An error in the original Earth Engine script omitted December in non-leap years and November 2026. The repair export supplied all 17 omitted December values (16 in 2003–2024, plus 2025). For November 2026, Earth Engine returned zero source images, so NDVI remains missing for that month as of the export. The required 2003–2024 index has all 264 values for all three variables. The GLDAS downloads end June 2026; July–November 2026 are `missing` locally, not necessarily unavailable from NASA. Missing values and unknown coverage are empty, never zero or interpolated. The quality report distinguishes the original 2003–2024 period from the requested extension.

Run `python scripts/process_global_gldas.py` followed by `python scripts/merge_global_timeseries.py` after installing `requirements-data.txt`; raw inputs remain under `data/raw`. The repair export is stored at `data/raw/modis_earth_engine/mod13a3_missing_months_repair.csv`. The source manifest hashes each MODIS aggregate export. November 2026 was absent from the Earth Engine collection when the repair was exported; check again later. The figures are data-quality visualizations, not trend analysis.

## Local validation audit

Run `python scripts/audit_member_b.py` to verify stored checksums and independently recompute surface and 0–100 cm GLDAS means for eight months spanning 2003, 2014 and 2024. The latest run verified 343 source/export checksums and 16 means; maximum absolute difference was 8.53e-13 kg m-2, with no negative layer cells in those sampled months. Across 286 valid MODIS aggregate rows, NDVI ranges from 0.3437 to 0.5098 and whole-Earth valid coverage from 0.1269 to 0.2078. Results are recorded in `validation_report.json`. Checksums do not independently authenticate NASA sources. This does not independently verify MODIS source pixels, global reduction or spatial overlap, and it is not comprehensive GLDAS scientific validation.
