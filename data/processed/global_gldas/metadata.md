# Whole-Earth GLDAS monthly data

Real NASA GLDAS_NOAH025_M.2.1 model output, not synthetic data. Region `whole_earth` is the full EPSG:4326 rectangle −180° to 180° longitude and −90° to 90° latitude, including oceans. GLDAS grid coverage is −60° to 90° latitude; missing ocean, ice and other unmodeled cells do not contribute to means. Thus these are means over available modeled cells, not measurements of the entire globe.

The input is 318 downloaded, subsetted NetCDF4 files, 2000-01 through 2026-06. Every file was opened and its filename, single monthly time coordinate, grid coordinates, three layers, `kg m-2` units, `time: mean` cell method and identity scaling were checked. SHA-256 checksums are in `source_manifest.json`. Full upstream granule provenance and scientific plausibility have not been independently established. Original per-file declared missing/fill values are masked using xarray CF decoding; in one inspected source the fill is −9999. No further scale or unit conversion is applied. Values are monthly means supplied by the source; no additional temporal averaging occurs.

`soil_water_0_10cm` uses `SoilMoi0_10cm_inst`. `soil_water_0_100cm` sums `SoilMoi0_10cm_inst`, `SoilMoi10_40cm_inst` and `SoilMoi40_100cm_inst` in each cell where all three are present. Both are water storage in kg m-2, not volumetric moisture; the 0–100 cm value is not universally root-zone moisture. Calculations weight 0.25° cells by spherical area based on latitude boundaries, radius 6,371,007.181 m, and normalize means by valid modeled area. `valid_area_fraction` divides valid modeled area by the whole sphere, including oceans. No interpolation or zero-filling of missing observations is performed.

The output explicitly indexes 2003-01 to 2026-11: 287 months per variable. The required 2003–2024 period contains 264 valid months for each variable. Downloaded sources end 2026-06; July–November 2026 are marked missing in this local dataset, without making a claim about their publication status. The CSV uses empty values and unknown coverage for missing source months. Present months have approximately 0.261 of Earth covered by modeled valid cells, and use `coverage_review_required` because a meaningful global coverage threshold is not yet agreed. A negative cell marks the affected monthly aggregate `negative_value`; missing or empty grids receive distinct flags. These checks are not comprehensive scientific QA.

The January 2003 area-weighted surface and total means were independently checked against direct cosine-latitude weighted numpy averages. The workflow is reproducible from repository root with `python -m pip install -r requirements-data.txt` and `python scripts/process_global_gldas.py`. The generated PNG is a visual check, not a trend analysis. Source files and monthly availability should be reassessed as additional months are downloaded.

For comparison, the MODIS January 2003 pilot has valid coverage of approximately 0.133 of Earth, versus GLDAS approximately 0.261. These are separate coverage fractions, not a measurement of geographical overlap. Different footprints mean the global mean series may describe different places; discuss this with the team before interpreting paired trends. The MODIS data have not yet passed independent source-pixel scientific verification; a combined dataset is available in `data/processed/global_combined`.
